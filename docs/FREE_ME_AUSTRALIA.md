# 🚀 Free Me

**Discover *your* path to financial freedom.**

A personalised financial-freedom journey for young Australians. You say what freedom means to you and
give your real numbers; Free Me turns both into a journey you can see, understand and follow — one clear
next step at a time, with the reasoning attached to every item.

Live, no sign-up needed: **https://hackathon-uqies-web.vercel.app**

---

## What it combines

Each of these exists on its own. None of them are together in one product.

| | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 🧠 | **An AI-generated plan** built from your situation, not a template |
| 🎓 | **Financial education** delivered when you need it, rewritten for your level |
| 🎯 | **Goal tracking** with real dates, amounts and honest projections |
| 🗺️ | **A visual journey** — places, connections, a destination that is yours |
| 📊 | **A serious dashboard** for people who don't want a world map |

## What makes it different

**1 · One plan, two faces.**
🎮 Explore is a world map. 📊 Professional is a plain dashboard. Same plan object, one toggle.
*Your journey doesn't change. How you experience it does.*

**2 · Bridges: your goals compete for the same dollars.**
Most apps treat goals as separate lists. Free Me draws the trade-off in your own situation —
*"money committed to long-term growth is money not available for a trip 18 months away."*

**3 · The maths is code. The AI only reasons.**
A rules engine computes every number you act on. The model receives those figures as facts and writes
the structure and the explanations. **It never does arithmetic that reaches you** — so every figure is
defensible.

**4 · Education, not advice — enforced in code.**
No product, ticker, fund, broker or coin is ever named. A validator rejects any plan that crosses the
line and falls back to a rules-only plan. Built to sit on the general-information side of the line ASIC
draws; a licensed review before public launch is on the roadmap.

## What it sounds like

Sarah, 21, student. $800 saved, $300/month spare, Japan in 18 months. The plan tells her she's behind —
and why:

> "You have $800 set aside, which covers about 0.53 months of your spending — a $2,200 gap to a two-month
> buffer of $3,000. A buffer is what lets you say yes to a trip without worrying."

Crypto gets one star out of five on her map, with the reason:

> "You describe yourself as moderate on risk and you have a trip with a fixed date 18 months out, and
> digital assets can swing far more than that timeframe allows."

For a 29-year-old with a deposit saved, it's two. Same product, different reading.

## Try it in two minutes

1. Open **https://hackathon-uqies-web.vercel.app** → *Open in Explore mode* (no account).

3. Open a place → **Why?** → set the buffer to $3,000 → a new path opens.
